06  ·  Principles & Ethics · Our Covenant

Open Science
Charter.

Every experiment is reproducible. Every system is auditable. Every dataset is documented. We build for accountability, not acclaim.

  • P-01Reproducibility First

    Every published result includes code, data, and instructions sufficient for independent reproduction. No cherry-picked benchmarks. No hidden hyperparameters. If it can't be reproduced, it isn't published.

  • P-02Open by Default

    All source code ships under Apache 2.0 or MIT. All documentation under CC BY 4.0. Proprietary dependencies are avoided. Vendor lock-in is treated as a technical defect.

  • P-03Safety as a Constraint, Not a Feature

    Systems deployed in safety-critical domains — healthcare, industrial, legal — are designed with fail-safe defaults, adversarial robustness testing, and formal verification where computationally feasible.

  • P-04Data Sovereignty & Privacy

    User data never leaves the deployment boundary without explicit consent. No telemetry by default. No training on user data. Systems are designed for on-premise and air-gapped deployment.

  • P-05Academic Independence

    Georbit operates as an independent research wing with non-profit governance. Research priorities are set by scientific merit and societal need, not commercial pressure or funding obligations.
